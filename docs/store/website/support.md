# Longnote Support

Email us at **support@novaralabs.in**. A person reads every message. We aim to reply within 2 working days.

Please include your Support ID (Longnote > Settings > Privacy) so we can find your records. Do not send us your recordings unless we ask.

## Cancel your subscription

On your iPhone, open **Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Longnote > Cancel Subscription**. You can also get there from **Longnote > Settings > Plan & billing**.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the free trial or the current period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

## Refunds

Apple handles all payments and refunds. We cannot issue refunds ourselves. Ask Apple for a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Restore a purchase

New phone, or reinstalled the app? Sign in with the same Apple ID, then open **Longnote > Settings > Restore purchases**. The same button is on the subscription screen.

## A note is stuck on processing, or failed

Your audio is safe on your phone. Check that you are online, open the note, and tap **Try again**. If it still does not finish, email us with your Support ID and the time you made the recording.

## Delete your data

Email support@novaralabs.in with your Support ID (**Longnote > Settings > Privacy**). We delete what our servers and our analytics provider hold for you and confirm within 30 days. To remove your notes from the phone, delete them in the app or delete the app.

To stop sharing analytics and ad measurement events, turn off **Settings > Share usage analytics**. To stop tracking, use **iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking**.
